CMRR Common-Mode Rejection Ratio
expand_more

A metric quantifying the capability of a differential biopotential amplifier to reject simultaneous noise signals occurring on both electrode inputs (e.g., ambient 50/60 Hz powerline hum) relative to differential neural/cardiac microvolt signals.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
CMRR = 20 × log10 ( | Ad | / | Acm | )
PHYSIOLOGICAL TARGET: > 100 dB (Clinical Grade)
fNyquist Nyquist-Shannon Sampling
expand_more

States that complete reconstruction of a continuous bandlimited analog biopotential waveform requires discrete digital sampling at more than double the maximum frequency component present in the signal, preventing spectral aliasing.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
fs > 2 × Bmax
EEG APPLICATION: fs ≥ 250 Hz (for 0.5–100 Hz Gamma)
RMSSD Root Mean Square Successive Diff
expand_more

The primary time-domain Heart Rate Variability (HRV) marker representing short-term beat-to-beat variation, heavily influenced by parasympathetic (vagal) autonomic regulation.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
RMSSD = √ [ 1/(N-1) × ∑ (RRi+1 − RRi)2 ]
RESTING NOMINAL RANGE: 20 – 75 ms (Age Dependent)
tdn Dicrotic Notch
expand_more

A small inflection point or transient dip observable on the catacrotic limb of the peripheral photoplethysmogram (PPG) contour, signifying aortic valve closure and retrograde flow dampening.

VASCULAR HEALTH METRIC
Reflectance Index: RI = (b/a) × 100%
MORPHOLOGY INDICATOR: Distinct in compliant elastic arteries
