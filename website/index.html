We also check your skin temperature. When both signals point the same way, the reading is more trustworthy.

Your heartbeat is not perfectly regular, and the pattern of tiny speed-ups and slow-downs shows whether your body is in "rest and digest" or "fight or flight" mode. This is the same idea behind stress scores on fitness watches.
